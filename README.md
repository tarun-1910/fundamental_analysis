# Equity Research & Fundamental Analysis

A structured approach to researching publicly listed companies and assessing their business quality, financial health, risks, and valuation.

## Overview

This project applies fundamental analysis to help investors understand how a company earns money, how its financial performance is changing, and what expectations may be reflected in its share price. It uses company filings and other reliable sources to support clear, evidence-based conclusions.

## Analysis Framework

- **Business analysis:** Review products, revenue sources, customers, industry trends, competitive position, and potential moat.
- **Financial analysis:** Assess revenue and earnings growth, margins, return ratios, debt, cash flow, working capital, and capital allocation.
- **Management and governance:** Review ownership, pledging, auditor opinions, related-party transactions, and material regulatory or governance developments.
- **Valuation:** Compare relevant valuation ratios with company history and peers; use scenario-based estimates where appropriate.
- **Risk assessment:** Identify company-specific risks and define indicators to monitor.

## Research Process

1. Identify the company, exchange listing, and latest available market data.
2. Review annual reports, quarterly results, investor presentations, and relevant exchange filings.
3. Cross-check important financial figures and record their reporting periods.
4. Evaluate business performance, financial position, governance, and risks.
5. Estimate valuation using explicit assumptions and explain key uncertainties.
6. Summarize the findings with a scorecard and reasoned conclusion.

## Example Applications

- Laurus Labs Ltd. — pharmaceutical manufacturing and CDMO analysis
- Billionbrains Garage Ventures Ltd. (Groww) — investment platform and financial-services analysis

## Limitations

Fundamental analysis relies on historical information and assumptions about future performance. Estimates can differ from actual outcomes, and valuation scenarios are not price predictions or guarantees of return. Research findings should not be treated as personalized financial advice.

## Disclaimer

This project is for educational and research purposes only. It is not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell securities. Investors should consider their own objectives, risk tolerance, and diversification, and may consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
